Clinical context

What is pneumonia?

Pneumonia is an infection or inflammatory condition affecting the air sacs in one or both lungs. These air sacs may fill with fluid or pus, making breathing more difficult.

Common symptoms may include cough, fever, chest discomfort, and difficulty breathing. Pneumonia can be caused by bacteria, viruses, and other organisms.

Project rationale

Why PneumoVision?

PneumoVision explores how deep learning can assist chest X-ray screening by providing rapid, model-based classification between NORMAL and PNEUMONIA.

It demonstrates an end-to-end machine learning workflow—from structured experimentation to a production-ready inference interface—while keeping clinical interpretation with qualified professionals.
